Our fraternity

Founded in friendship

Omega Psi Phi began at Howard University in Washington, D.C., on November 17, 1911. Edgar Amos Love, Oscar James Cooper, and Frank Coleman founded the fraternity with faculty adviser Ernest Everett Just.

Its motto is “Friendship is Essential to the Soul.”

Our chapter

From 33 men to a lasting legacy

In spring 1995, 33 Omega men began organizing a new Baton Rouge chapter. Early planning included meetings in members’ homes, with Bradford Edwards and Michael Jones helping lead the effort.

Lambda Kappa Kappa received its charter on December 7, 1995, becoming the fraternity’s 890th chapter.
